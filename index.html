About Me

Hi, I'm James Traynor, an aspiring full-stack web developer with a deep curiosity for how things work — especially in the digital world. What started as casual tinkering with HTML has turned into a focused journey toward building responsive, accessible, and user-friendly web experiences. I'm currently sharpening my frontend skills with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React while preparing to dive into backend development with Node.js and Express.

I enjoy blending creativity with logic — whether it's designing clean layouts, debugging tricky code, or making interfaces that feel smooth and intuitive. Learning to code has become more than just a skill for me — it's a mindset. I take pride in learning by doing: building projects, solving problems, and always pushing to improve my understanding of how modern applications come together from top to bottom.

Outside of code, I'm the kind of person who's always looking for the next challenge, whether it's building my own simulation-style game project or finding better ways to manage my workflow. I believe the best developers never stop learning — and I'm all in. This journey isn't just about mastering the tools, it's about becoming the kind of developer who can bring real ideas to life.
